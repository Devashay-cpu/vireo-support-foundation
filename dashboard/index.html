1. The story: a Pulse 2 lot defect, not a support-team collapse

CSAT fell from about 3.5 to a trough of 2.95 in 2026-02 as tickets from Pulse 2 lots PL2-2510/2511/2512 rose from 0.3% to 40% of volume. Those lots run at 31.9 replacements per 100 orders vs 5.1 for other Pulse 2 lots.

4. Training priority

Train now: A3004 (Siddharth Kapoor), A3006 (Kavya Pandey), A3005 (Zaid Khanna), A3007 (Siddharth Trivedi): the only agents whose mix-adjusted gap excludes zero. The other six raw-bottom-ten agents show no training signal; their low raw score reflects Delivery/Returns queues. Closing the four gaps lifts Tier 1 CSAT by about 0.035.

5. Business impact and goal

  • Rs 1,184,099 measured direct cost: 531 excess replacements at Rs 1,820 (unit cost + Rs 340, policy s5) plus 790 excess contacts at about Rs 277. Lower bound: order-linked tickets only.
  • Goal: blended CSAT 3.33 → at least 3.42, held two quarters, by end Q1 2027. Defect-lot tickets are 13.8% of responses at 2.77 vs 3.42 elsewhere: 0.644 × 0.138 = 0.089.

Limitations

Association, not proven cause. Only 65% of Pulse 2 tickets link to an order/lot. No case-severity field, so the four agents' gap may partly reflect harder cases. CSAT is respondents only (44%). No revenue data, so CSAT loss is not monetised. Full detail: analysis/business_analysis.md.
